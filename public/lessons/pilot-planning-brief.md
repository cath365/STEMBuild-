# STEMBuild: classroom pilot planning brief

STEMBuild is an independent project by Emmanuel Inambao, Zambia.
Stage: MVP development and validation. No classroom impact results are claimed.

## Proposed scope (to agree with a participating school)
- Duration: four to six weeks.
- Lesson: Smart Environment Monitor with Arduino Uno or classic ESP32.
- Confirm school, teacher lead, age group, number of learners and available kits.
- Verify accounts, published content, consent, safeguarding and data-handling arrangements before enrolment.
- Agree start/end dates, technical support and success criteria.

## Measures
- Comparable before/after practical tasks and criterion-level rubric scores.
- Teacher-reviewed build evidence and learner explanations.
- Attempts, troubleshooting and unresolved faults.
- Teacher review time and qualitative usability feedback.
- Offline access, sync outcomes and lost-work reports with device/connectivity context.

Report sample size, missing data, support given, assessment consistency and limitations.
A small uncontrolled pilot does not establish broad causal impact.

## Costs to estimate before agreeing a funding ask
- Shared kit components, shipping and replacement parts.
- Hosting, storage, connectivity and technical support.
- Teacher preparation and content review.
- Evaluation and privacy safeguards.
