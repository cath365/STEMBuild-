# STEMBuild MVP decisions

## MVP success criterion
A teacher can assign a practical lesson, a student can complete the lesson on a supported board, submit evidence plus troubleshooting attempts, and the teacher can score the work against a rubric. The dashboard then exposes the learner's practical-skill gap separately from quiz performance.

## Explicit non-goals for v0.1
- Live serial telemetry from microcontrollers.
- Automatic computer-vision grading of wiring.
- Remote firmware flashing from the browser.
- Social/community features.
- Paid subscriptions, school billing or marketplace features.
- Claims of pilot impact or learning improvement without collected evidence.

## Analytics definitions
- **Quiz performance:** scored quiz attempts / available quiz score.
- **Practical performance:** teacher-awarded rubric points / available rubric points.
- **Attempts:** explicit quiz attempts and practical re-submissions/troubleshooting entries.
- **Completion rate:** completed required learning items / assigned required items.
- **Skill gap:** learning outcome or rubric criterion with repeated low scores or incomplete evidence; this is descriptive, not an AI diagnosis.
